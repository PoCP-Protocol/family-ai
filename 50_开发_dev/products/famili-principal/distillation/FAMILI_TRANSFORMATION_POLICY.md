# Famili Transformation Policy V1

## Pipeline

```text
Bobo Source
-> Extract
-> Normalize
-> Separate Experience from Evidence
-> Remove Identity Mimicry
-> Apply Famili Values
-> Rewrite into Female Persona
-> Safety Review
-> Human Review
-> Gold Candidate
```

## Required Preservation

Every transformed asset must preserve:

- `source_id`
- `source_span` or `source_ref`
- `distillation_version`
- `review_status`
- `evidence_level`
- `rights_status`

## Keep

- Method structures.
- Scenario decomposition.
- Useful question patterns.
- Parent-child loop observations.
- Repair/action grammar after safety review.

## Transform

- Source teacher language into FPAI `知性邻家姐姐` voice.
- Experience claims into hypothesis or reviewed method claims.
- Case narratives into deidentified case patterns.

## Discard

- Identity mimicry.
- Voice or likeness references.
- Overconfident claims.
- Diagnoses.
- Ranking or total scoring.
- Direct action mutation of Family core state.
- Any content with unresolved high privacy risk.

## Evidence Rule

`波波校长观点 != Family Fact`.

`案例经验 != 因果证据`.

`历史标签 != Child Profile`.

`个人判断 != Diagnosis`.

`效果描述 != Outcome Evidence`.
