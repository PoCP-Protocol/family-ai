# Bobo Teacher Model V1

BTM is a source-teacher abstraction for extracting reusable family-education methods. It is not a model checkpoint, not a persona clone, and not a monolithic prompt.

## Asset Categories

### KNOWLEDGE

Themes, concepts, parent-child interaction frames, and education knowledge that can be source-traced and reviewed.

### METHOD

Reusable intervention families such as connection before correction, reducing defensiveness, repair before teaching, parent self-regulation, and small-action-first.

### SCENARIO

Common family scenes where parent and child enter a repeating loop: screen time, homework drag, defiance, low motivation, parent blowup, sibling attention, and education disagreement.

### DECISION_PATTERN

What the teacher tends to notice before advising: risk level, child age, relationship temperature, parent emotional load, escalation point, and whether immediate safety overrides coaching.

### LANGUAGE_PATTERN

Useful ways to lower shame and defensiveness. These patterns must be transformed into FPAI voice and must not mimic personal catchphrases or identity markers.

### CASE_PATTERN

Abstracted case structure: trigger, parent interpretation, child reaction, escalation loop, repair opening, one small action, review signal.

## Extraction Questions

- What does the teacher notice first?
- What does the teacher ask before advising?
- What interpretation is offered as possibility rather than fact?
- Which intervention family is chosen?
- What language lowers defensiveness?
- What actions are avoided because they increase shame, conflict, or risk?
- Which claims are weak evidence and need review?

## Weak Evidence Zones

- Experience-based claims about effectiveness.
- Anecdotal case outcomes.
- Strong claims about child psychology without independent evidence.
- Source material with unclear rights or personal/minor data risk.

## Prohibitions

- Do not create a single Bobo system prompt.
- Do not imitate Bobo identity, voice, likeness, or authority.
- Do not use unreviewed source text as SFT target.
- Do not treat historical labels as Child Profile facts.
