## Product Intent

`法咪莉校长` is a real digital-human IP track, not merely a text chatbot and not a visual skin for another product. The IP must become a coherent AI person: someone a parent can ask, hear, watch, learn from, and return to across family-growth situations.

The core identity is a `知性邻家姐姐型家庭成长 AI 校长`: clear, warm, practical, bounded, and able to teach without scolding.

## DH0 Authorized Work

- IP Bible: identity, values, temperament, relational role, boundaries, and recurring teaching motifs.
- Visual direction: silhouette, age impression, wardrobe language, color world, scene grammar, and expression range.
- Non-cloned voice direction: pace, warmth, register, sentence rhythm, and prohibited voice references.
- Teaching presence: how she explains, demonstrates, asks follow-up questions, and closes with one small action.
- Multimodal experience map: text, voice, avatar, micro lesson, classroom, companion, and reviewer surfaces.
- Prototype acceptance criteria: what a future voice/avatar prototype must prove before production.
- Rights and safety blockers: what must be cleared before any production digital-human launch.

## DH0 Prohibited Work

- Do not clone Bobo's face, voice, likeness, identity, endorsement, or private persona.
- Do not clone any real person's face, voice, likeness, identity, endorsement, or private persona.
- Do not train SFT, LoRA, voice, avatar, or lip-sync models.
- Do not use unknown-rights raw corpus as training or production retrieval material.
- Do not launch public digital-human runtime.
- Do not wire FPAI into Family M2 runtime.

## Gate

DH0 passes only when the IP can be specified without real-person cloning, can be tested as a multimodal experience concept, and has explicit blockers before voice/avatar/runtime production.

DH0 is a horizontal IP Foundation track. It is not FP3 runtime, not DH1, and not a vendor/technology selection stage.
