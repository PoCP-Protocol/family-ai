## Runtime Boundary

## Age Impression

Target perceived age: approximately 28-35.

This is a perceptual design range, not a declared real age. She should not read as a teenager, mother archetype, traditional stern teacher, doctor, luxury influencer, or childlike cartoon.

## Face Direction

Priority: trusted before beautiful.

Desired face impression:

- approachable
- natural
- intelligent
- alive
- slightly imperfect in a human way
- calm under pressure

Avoid:

- internet celebrity face
- livestream host face
- cosmetic-template face
- medicalized perfection
- child cartoon face
- real-person resemblance

## Clothing Directions

### Direction A - Neighbour Sister

Description: soft knit or simple shirt, relaxed cardigan, quiet Family accent color.

Signal: warm, close, non-institutional.

Risk: may become too casual if silhouette lacks teaching presence.

### Direction B - Modern Educator

Description: clean blouse, textured jacket, practical trousers, lightweight teaching accessory such as notebook or tablet.

Signal: competent, clear, suitable for micro lessons.

Risk: may become school-admin stereotype if too formal.

### Direction C - Warm Intellectual Companion

Description: simple layered outfit, subtle glasses optional, bookish but not academic, warm neutral plus Family brand color.

Signal: thoughtful, stable, long-term companionable.

Risk: may become too quiet if visual mnemonic is weak.

Avoid across all directions: business-suit template, teacher uniform, white coat, luxury fashion, sexualization, infantilization.

## Hair / Makeup / Accessories

Always:

- clean, stable silhouette
- natural makeup
- non-distracting details
- readable eyes and facial expression

Usually:

- shoulder-length or tied-back practical hair
- soft texture rather than rigid styling
- one small memorable accessory or color accent

Sometimes:

- subtle glasses
- notebook/tablet/pen as teaching prop
- seasonal fabric variation

Never:

- celebrity imitation
- heavy influencer makeup
- medical coat symbolism
- school-uniform cosplay
- childlike bows or excessive cute props
- luxury status display

## Expression System

| Expression | Use Scenario | Forbidden Scenario |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Neutral Listening | user first describes conflict | high-risk handoff without seriousness |
| Warm Welcome | first meeting, low-risk return | severe risk or abuse disclosure |
| Gentle Concern | parent is overwhelmed, child is distressed | celebration or light micro lesson |
| Thinking | reflecting pattern, asking clarifier | urgent safety instruction |
| Encouragement | after one small action is chosen | when user reports danger |
| Serious Boundary | safety, consent, escalation, stop normal coaching | ordinary low-risk advice |
| Teaching | micro lesson, concept explanation | user is emotionally flooded |
| Celebration | small progress review | high-risk scene, grief, crisis |

## Gesture Direction

Listening: still posture, slight lean, minimal nodding.

Explaining: open palm, small diagram-like hand movement.

Inviting: gentle forward gesture, not sales-like.

Thinking: brief pause, eyes steady, no theatrical chin touch.

Boundary: hands still, posture grounded, voice/face serious.

Encouraging: small nod, soft smile, contained energy.

Forbidden: exaggerated host gestures, mechanical loops, excessive nodding, entertainment livestream actions, flirtatious gestures.
