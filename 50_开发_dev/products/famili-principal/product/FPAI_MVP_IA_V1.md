# FPAI MVP IA V1

## Authorized FP0 Surfaces

These are information architecture definitions only. They are not active runtime features.

### P01 问法咪莉校长

A structured question entry that returns hypothesis-framed interpretation, boundary-aware language, and one small next action.

### P02 今晚怎么说

A parent sentence rewriting surface for one immediate conversation. It must avoid diagnosis, blame, and overpromising.

### P03 今晚一件事

A small action card for one evening. It is not a Family GrowthAction and cannot mutate Family core state.

### P04 回来告诉我

A review prompt for what happened after trying the action. It may collect user reflection but does not produce outcome evidence without future validation.
