# FPAI Roadmap V1

## Product Destination

`法咪莉校长` is a multimodal digital-human IP for family growth: an interactive AI principal that can eventually teach, converse, guide practice, and appear across text, voice, avatar, classroom, and companion experiences.

The roadmap is staged so the Soul, text intelligence core, and digital-human IP foundation can advance without prematurely activating cloning, production avatar runtime, or public multimodal launch.

## DH0 - Digital-Human IP Foundation

Status: AUTHORIZED.

Scope: create the Family-owned `法咪莉校长` digital-human IP Bible, visual direction, non-cloned voice direction, multimodal experience map, prototype acceptance criteria, and asset rights/provenance system for future prototype implementation.

Not in scope: voice cloning, likeness cloning, production avatar runtime, lip sync, public launch, model training, or Family M2 runtime integration.

DH0 can run in parallel with FP1 because it is an IP design and acceptance track. DH0 is not FP3 runtime and does not choose voice/avatar vendors or generate production assets.

## FP0 - Soul & Corpus Foundation

Status: PASS_INTERNAL.

Outcome: establish product boundary, corpus governance, persona DNA, BTM/FPS distillation rules, response contracts, safety policy, and eval scaffolding. No training or runtime launch.

## FP1 - Prototype Retrieval & Structured Response

Status: AUTHORIZED_TEXT_INTELLIGENCE_MVP.

Scope: reviewed retrieval over approved method/knowledge cards, structured response generation behind replaceable model gateway, contract validation, safety routing, and eval.

## FP2 - Companion Workflow

Status: NOT_AUTHORIZED.

Possible future scope: 21-day companion, longitudinal check-ins, and outcome capture. This cannot begin without FP1 gate, consent design, and Human Gate policy.

## FP3 - Voice / Avatar / Digital Human

Status: NOT_AUTHORIZED_FOR_RUNTIME_IMPLEMENTATION.

Future scope: implement public-facing multimodal IP expression after explicit owner authorization, rights review, safety review, and DH0 acceptance review.

DH0 may prepare the IP and prototype requirements. Voice cloning, likeness cloning, lip sync, real-time avatar, and digital-human production runtime remain outside FP1/DH0 and require explicit rights, consent, safety, and owner approval.
