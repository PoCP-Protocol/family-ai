## Product Boundary

`We are 伐木累` is the Family-facing community, content, challenge, lifestyle, and brand entrance. It is not a generic forum and is not a direct Family Core state writer.

Family is the platform and Growth OS. `法咪莉校长` is the AI companion entrance. `We are 伐木累` is the community and participation entrance.

```text
Family = FAMILY_GROWTH_OS
Famili Principal AI = AI_COMPANION_ENTRANCE
We are Famili = COMMUNITY_CONTENT_CHALLENGE_BRAND
```

## Current Phase

WF0 = Product Definition.
WF1 = Content + Challenge Community MVP.

Authorized work:

- Brand and product positioning.
- Information architecture.
- Relationship with Family and Famili Principal AI.
- Community state boundary.
- Consent and safety boundary.
- Challenge loop design.
- WF1 candidate MVP scope.
- WAF community prototype as a Family 1.0 consumer slice.
- Commercial validation track from first real families.

Not authorized in WF1:

- Membership runtime implementation.
- Family M2 runtime integration.
- Direct writes to Family Core state.
- Recommendation or ranking platform.
- Public child growth profile exposure.
